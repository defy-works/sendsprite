## Signing

We use [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/contributor-assistant/github-action).
The first time you open a pull request, a bot comments with a link and a
one-line statement to post back. That comment is your signature: it records your
GitHub username, your commit author identity and the timestamp, and it covers
every pull request you open afterwards. Nothing to print, scan or email.

**Signing as an individual.** Comment on the pull request as instructed. If your
employer owns work you do — check your employment contract, it is more common
than people expect — either get their sign-off or ask them to sign as an entity.

**Signing as an entity.** Email <legal@defy.works> from a company address, with:

- the legal name of the organisation and its registered address,
- the name, title and email of the person authorised to sign,
- the GitHub usernames of the people who will contribute (send updates as this
  list changes).

We countersign and record it, and contributions from those accounts are covered
from then on.
