# Sendsprite trademark policy

The code is open source. The name is not.

"Sendsprite", the Sendsprite wordmark and the Sendsprite logo are trademarks of
defy.works. The licences in `LICENSE` and `packages/*/LICENSE` cover copyright
in the software; they do not grant any right to use these marks. That is
deliberate, and it is the only thing we hold back: it keeps "Sendsprite" meaning
one thing, so nobody has to guess whether the instance in front of them is ours.

## What you can do without asking

- **Run it, anywhere, under any name.** Self-hosting Sendsprite — for yourself,
  your company, or your customers — needs no permission and no name change.
- **Fork it and modify it.** The licence says so and the trademark does not take
  it back.
- **Say true things about it.** Nominative use is fine:
  - "compatible with Sendsprite"
  - "a fork of Sendsprite"
  - "based on Sendsprite"
  - "we host Sendsprite for you"
  - "Sendsprite" in a comparison, review, article, talk or benchmark
- **Keep the marks in an unmodified copy.** Redistributing Sendsprite as it
  came, still called Sendsprite, is what the marks are for.

## What needs a different name

If you publish a **modified** version, or offer a **public service** built on
Sendsprite, give it your own name and your own logo. Concretely:

- Do not name your product, company, service or app "Sendsprite", or anything
  close enough to be mistaken for it ("Sendsprite Cloud", "Sendsprite Pro",
  "SendSprite.io").
- Do not use the wordmark or logo as your own branding, favicon or app icon.
- Do not register "Sendsprite" (or a confusable variant) as a domain, package
  name, social handle or trademark.
- Do not imply we endorse, sponsor, certify or support you.

"Powered by Sendsprite" underneath your own name is fine. "Sendsprite" as your
name is not.

## Asking

Anything not covered above — bundling the logo, a partner or integration badge,
a name that sits close to the line — just ask. Open an issue or email
<legal@defy.works> with what you want to do and where it will appear. We answer
in good faith and we would much rather say yes with a condition than send a
letter.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Doing so does not retroactively make your past use
infringing, and it never changes the software licence: the code stays under the
terms in `LICENSE` and `packages/*/LICENSE` whatever happens here.
