# Privacy Policy

**Effective 28 August 2026.** This policy explains how **defy.works** ("we",
"us") handles personal data on the hosted Sendsprite service at
**sendsprite.com**. It is written to be read in one sitting; if anything is
unclear, ask at [hello@defy.works](mailto:hello@defy.works).

## Two kinds of data, two roles

The Service handles personal data in two capacities, and the distinction
matters for what we do with it and who you should contact.

- **Account data** — information about you and your team as users of the
  Service. Here we decide why and how the data is processed: we are the
  **controller**.
- **Customer data** — the recipients, contacts, email content and events that
  you put into the Service in order to send email. Here you decide why the
  data is processed and we act on your instructions: you are the controller and
  we are your **processor**. If you are a recipient of an email sent through
  Sendsprite and want to know why you received it or have it stop, contact the
  sender; they are the ones holding your consent.

## Self-hosted instances

Sendsprite is open source. This policy covers only the instance we run at
sendsprite.com. An instance run by anyone else is that operator's
responsibility, and we receive no data from it.

## Account data we collect

| Data                                                                   | Where it comes from                    | Why                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email address, password hash                                     | Sign-up form                           | Creating and securing your account                            |
| Google or GitHub profile (id, email, name, avatar)                     | The provider, when you sign in with it | Signing you in without a password                             |
| Session IP address and user agent                                      | Your browser or client                 | Keeping you signed in, spotting stolen sessions               |
| Audit log (action, actor, IP, user agent, time)                        | Actions in the dashboard and API       | Letting team admins see who changed what; abuse investigation |
| Team name, members, invitations, settings                              | You and your team                      | Running the team                                              |
| API key names and hashed secrets                                       | Keys you create                        | Authenticating API, SMTP, CLI and MCP requests                |
| AWS credentials or stack identifiers, Cloudflare OAuth tokens          | Connections you make in Settings       | Sending through your SES account; writing DNS records for you |
| Billing state (plan, subscription id, usage counts, Polar customer id) | Polar and our own metering             | Charging the right amount and enforcing plan limits           |
| Support email                                                          | You, when you write to us              | Answering you                                                 |

AWS credentials and Cloudflare tokens are encrypted at rest with a key that is
not stored alongside the database. We never see your card number: payment
details go directly to Polar.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the dashboard.
The only cookies are the ones that keep you signed in.

## Customer data we process for you

- **Contacts and contact books**: email addresses, names and any merge fields
  you attach.
- **Emails**: sender, recipients, subject, headers, body and attachments, and
  the delivery events SES reports back (sent, delivered, bounced, complained,
  rejected).
- **Tracking**: when open or click tracking is on (the default for a new team,
  changeable per team and per message) the Service records that a tracking
  pixel was loaded or a rewritten link was followed, together with the
  requesting IP address and user agent. Turn tracking off in **Settings** or
  per message if you do not want this.
- **Suppressions and unsubscribes**: addresses that bounced, complained or
  unsubscribed, so you stop sending to them.
- **Webhook deliveries**: the payloads and responses of events sent to your
  endpoints.

We process customer data only to provide the Service as you have configured
it, and never for our own marketing or to build profiles of recipients.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except
where the law requires it or to defend our rights.

## International transfers

We are based in the Republic of Korea and our providers operate in the EU and
the United States, so data moves across borders as part of providing the
Service. Where a transfer needs a legal safeguard we rely on the provider's
standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism.

## How long we keep it

- **Email bodies and attachments** are purged by a nightly job once they are
  older than your team's retention setting (**Settings → Retention**), which
  is capped by the instance maximum. Metadata — sender, recipients, subject,
  events — stays until you delete the email or the team, so the dashboard and
  API history continue to work.
- **Webhook delivery logs** follow the same retention window.
- **Contacts, templates, campaigns, suppressions** stay until you delete them.
- **Sessions** end when you sign out, when a team admin removes you, or when
  they expire.
- **Audit log** is kept for the life of the team.
- **Deleting a team** removes its domains, keys, contacts, emails, events and
  settings. Deleting your account (contact us) removes your personal profile;
  teams you own must be deleted or handed over first.
- **Billing records** are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires,
  in Polar's systems and ours.

Backups may retain copies for up to 30 days after deletion.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export,
restrict or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain
to a supervisory authority (in Korea, the Personal Information Protection
Commission). Most of this you can do yourself in the dashboard — profile,
sessions, team deletion, contact export. For anything else, email
[hello@defy.works](mailto:hello@defy.works) and we will respond within 30
days.

If your request concerns customer data — you received an email through
Sendsprite and want to know why — we will forward it to the sender, who is the
controller, and help them respond.

## Security

Passwords are hashed; credentials for AWS and Cloudflare are encrypted at rest;
all traffic to the Service is over TLS; API keys are shown once and stored
hashed. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the
Service. If we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you without
undue delay and, where the law requires, the relevant authority.

## Children

The Service is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us
and we will delete it.

## Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, notify you by email or in
the dashboard at least 14 days before they take effect.
